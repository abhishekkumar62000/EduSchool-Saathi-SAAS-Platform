1. BRAND / PRODUCT POSITIONING

==================================================

Product Name:

EduSchool-Saathi

Business Type:

Multi-School School Management SaaS Platform

Category:

Education Technology / EdTech / School Management SaaS

Core Concept:

EduSchool-Saathi is a common digital platform that allows multiple independent schools to manage their complete school operations from one modern software platform.

The platform follows a multi-tenant SaaS architecture concept:

EduSchool-Saathi Super Admin

        ↓

Multiple Schools

        ↓

Each School has its own isolated environment

        ↓

School Admin / Director

        ↓

Teachers / Staff

        ↓

Students

        ↓

Parents

Each school must be presented as completely independent.

School A must never see School B's information.

EduSchool-Saathi provides schools with a centralized, simple and affordable digital management solution without requiring each school to build expensive custom software.

Target market:

- Schools in Madhubani

- Darbhanga

- Bihar

- Rural areas

- Semi-urban areas

- Growing private schools

- Public/private educational institutions

- Schools that are still dependent on registers, spreadsheets, WhatsApp and manual processes

Core positioning:

"From manual school management to one connected digital platform."
